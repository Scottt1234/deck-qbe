Why Now
Four forces that make this
the moment to act
The capability QBE needs already exists. What’s changed is the deadline — and the size of the prize.
Advertising Studio is retiring
Subscriptions stop renewing beyond 15 August 2026, and the product expires shortly after. Every audience built on it now has to be rebuilt later — the clock is already running.
Growth can’t come from media alone
Tripling premiums on a modest media budget means converting demand, not just buying impressions. The maths only works if existing demand is captured far more efficiently.
The data is already there
Policy, quote, web and portal signals already exist across the business, waiting to be joined up. The raw material for smarter targeting is sitting inside QBE today.
Brand and advocacy are an edge
A trusted brand and a loyal base let QBE win the right audiences with the right message in the right channel. Used well, advocacy becomes the cheapest growth channel you have.
The Gap
How marketing runs today vs.
how it could run tomorrow
The shift is from channel-by-channel campaigns to data-driven, moment-based marketing across every touchpoint.
Today
With a unified data foundation
Spray and pay. Paid media reaches existing policyholders and cold prospects alike, wasting spend and diluting the customer experience.
Suppress and target. Existing customers are suppressed, in-market intent audiences are built from real behaviour, and lookalikes seed on predicted value.
Channel silos. Email, paid and web are run separately, by separate logic, with no shared view of the customer or the journey.
One customer, every channel. Web, portal, email and paid media draw on a single unified profile — targeting and messaging stay consistent everywhere.
Disconnected web. Quote-tool and portal behaviour never reaches marketing, so high-intent visitors look the same as everyone else.
Behaviour drives the journey. A repeat visit to car-insurance pages or an abandoned quote triggers the next best action — in real time.
